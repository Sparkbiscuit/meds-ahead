# App icon provenance

The production icon was generated with OpenAI's built-in image generation tool, then integrated as 1024-by-1024 regular, dark, and tinted iOS icon assets. No stock imagery or third-party marks are used.

## Regular icon prompt

> Create a premium, distinctly native iOS app icon for a private medication supply and refill manager called Meds. Use a restrained abstract capsule as the central symbol, paired with a subtle circular supply-runway arc that suggests time and remaining inventory. Deep indigo, rich blue, and sea-glass cyan; luminous layered depth; soft glass-like material; crisp silhouette; elegant Apple-quality finish; centered composition; no text, letters, numbers, medical cross, heart, snake, bottle label, notification badge, watermark, or device mockup. Square 1024x1024 app icon artwork with edge-to-edge background and enough visual weight to remain legible at small sizes.

## Tinted icon prompt

> Convert this exact app icon into a strong monochrome iOS tinted-icon variant. Preserve the capsule and supply-arc composition and proportions exactly. Use only black, white, and grayscale luminosity with a transparent-looking tonal hierarchy suitable for Apple's user-selected tint rendering. No text, added objects, gradients that depend on hue, watermark, border, or device mockup. Keep the silhouette exceptionally clear at small sizes.
