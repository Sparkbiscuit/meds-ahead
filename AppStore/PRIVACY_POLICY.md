# Meds privacy policy

Effective date: August 23, 2026

Meds stores the medication details, schedules, dose logs, supply changes, and settings you enter locally on your iPhone. The app does not create an account, display advertising, include third-party analytics, track you, or send medication information to the developer or another server.

Label text and supported barcodes are recognized on device. Imported or captured images are processed for recognition and are not retained by Meds. A scanned web address is never opened automatically.

Notifications are scheduled by iOS on your device. Detailed notification text is optional and off by default.

Meds configures its local medication database not to be included in device backups.

You can delete individual dose logs or delete a medication and its associated local history in the app. Deleting the app removes its local data according to iOS behavior.

Meds is an organization tool, not a medical service. It does not provide medical advice, recommend dose changes, or determine refill eligibility.

Questions about privacy can be sent to nick@christoforakis.com. Do not include prescription-label photos or medication details unless you intentionally choose to disclose them.
